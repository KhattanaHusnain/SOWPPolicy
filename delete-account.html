Request Account Deletion

This page explains how users of apps developed by Husnain Mujahid (including SalatTracker and SOWProgramming) can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account and data, send an email to:
📧 mujahid.husnain.56@gmail.com
with the subject line: Account Deletion Request, and the name of the App.

Please include the email address associated with your account so we can verify ownership. Once verified, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

What Data is Deleted

  • Personal account information (name, email, profile data).
  • App-specific data tied to your account (for example, activity logs, saved progress, or synced backups, depending on the App).

What Data May Be Retained

We may retain some information for legal, security, or compliance reasons, such as anonymized logs or financial records. Such data cannot be linked back to you.
